About me

The person behind your project.

Julius Njuguna founded Julisa Infrastructure Limited to build the roads and civil works that communities and businesses depend on. He leads the company on a simple standard: do the work properly, keep the site safe, and finish what was promised.

Clients deal directly with the person accountable for their project, from the first site visit to handover.

Services

Three disciplines, one accountable team.

Tell us the job. We assess the site, price it clearly and build it.

Earthmoving

Heavy plant and skilled operators for moving and shaping ground.

  • Site clearing and stripping
  • Bulk excavation, cut and fill
  • Loading, hauling and grading
  • Backfilling and compaction

Road construction

Durable roads built on well-prepared formation with proper drainage.

  • Road bed preparation and grading
  • Gravel and murram roads
  • Bituminous surfacing
  • Drainage and road furniture

Civil works

Structures and drainage that keep roads and sites working.

  • Culverts and drainage channels
  • Bridges and structures
  • Retaining walls and foundations
  • Site infrastructure works

How we work

From first call to handover.

  1. Site assessment

    We visit the site, review the drawings and understand what you need built.

  2. Plan and quote

    You receive a clear scope, programme and price in writing.

  3. Build

    Crews and plant mobilise. You get regular progress updates.

  4. Handover

    We inspect the finished work with you and close out the job.
